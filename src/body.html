Before you start

Please read this once. It matters.

{{APP_NAME}} is a wellness and fitness tool. It is not a medical device.

  • It is for general wellness, training and education only.
  • Do not use it to diagnose, treat, rule out or monitor any illness or heart condition, or to make decisions about medicines or treatment.
  • It has not been tested, cleared or approved by any medical regulator. A chest strap records one ECG lead: many heart problems cannot be seen with it, and automated results can be wrong.
  • A normal-looking report does not mean your heart is healthy. An unusual one is most often caused by the strap or movement, not by your heart.
  • Reference ranges are for adults.
  • If you feel unwell, with chest pain or pressure, fainting, severe breathlessness, or a racing or very irregular heartbeat, call your local emergency number now. Do not stop to record.
  • For any health concern, see a doctor. Do not rely on this app.

Recording guidance
  • Moisten the electrodes and fit the strap snugly, just below the chest muscles.
  • Close Polar Beat, Polar Flow and any other app connected to the strap; the H10 streams ECG to one app at a time.
  • For a morning baseline, record on waking, before caffeine, in the same position each day, for the same duration (5 minutes recommended). Breathe normally.
  • For overnight recordings, keep the device on charge with this page in the foreground. The screen stays awake; use Dim screen to darken it.
  • If Bluetooth drops, the app reconnects automatically and marks the missing period in the report.
  • Data is saved to the device every minute, so an interrupted recording can still be analyzed.
